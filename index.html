Four tracks, one operator

V1 — EDIT

Video Editing

Long-form video, Instagram reels, and paid social ads. Pacing, sound design, colour, and delivery specs tuned to where the piece is actually going to run.

LX — GAFFER

Set logistics, equipment runs, and general on-the-ground support — filling gaps so the crew stays focused and the day stays on schedule.
